# Living Everdell prototype

Two players, 48 card types and the base Everdell rules engine

Hand cards fan out at the bottom. Eight market miniatures, two freely arranged cities and worker locations surround the ancient tree

Hover an object or its fixed city foundation to inspect its card. Select a playable hand card or market object, then choose a free position in the appropriate city

Free camera: drag, right drag to rotate, wheel to zoom, WASD and Q/E

All six supplies and the next-season forecast remain visible at both settlements. Choose distinct worker species at setup

Seasons are shared: a player may pass, then the other may continue. Advance only after both pass or run out of legal actions, resolving production choices before the shared landscape changes. No free workers alone does not end a season while card actions remain

Production circles show icon rewards and exchanges, with remaining / total places for limited locations. Locks explain unavailable actions on hover

Snow, flowers and autumn foliage follow the season. Market residents wander, recruited residents walk into the city, and animal porters carry new buildings. Escape opens the game menu

Tabletop archive remains available at archive/tabletop-pages-12a/
